 Three weeks ago, we released Phonic-1. Today, we're introducing Phonic-1.1, our latest speech-to-speech model.

 Phonic-1.1 is the new leader on Artificial Analysis's Speech to Speech Index , improving 1.8 points over Phonic-1, and also tops other industry benchmarks such as Sierra's τ³-Voice and David AI's DIAL.

 Ranks are positions on the full index, which also lists other versions and settings of these models.

 Turn-taking
 Phonic-1.1 is significantly better at understanding when to speak and when to listen. It waits for the speaker to finish, even when they pause mid-thought, and it holds up in noisy environments with other voices in the background.
 On DIAL , which holds live, multi-turn conversations with human raters, Phonic-1.1 has the lowest turn-taking defect rate and ranks first :
