 Investor Relations 

 Openness is what turns a technology into an ecosystem. A handful of gifted people can invent a technology or build a tool. Only when people share what they've built and work together as a network can creativity flourish and change the way we live.
 AI ecosystems start with open source. In the 100 days since we open-sourced MiniMax H3, developers have turned one H3 into 1,472 derivative models for 1,472 distinct needs and uses. Together, these models have been downloaded more than 148 million times, an average of over 17 downloads a second. New derivatives continue to appear.
 H3 has given enthusiasts, developers, and companies a common starting point. Leading technology companies such as NVIDIA, Tencent, and Alibaba have trained their own versions of H3 for different purposes. So have AI application and platform leaders including fal, Suno, HeyGen, Pika, and Creatify, and research teams at MIT, the National University of Singapore, and The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. More than ten leading companies worldwide now have licensing partnerships with MiniMax. They're using H3 to strengthen production workflows in e-commerce, gaming, film and TV, animation and comics, short-form dramas, industrial design, and other fields.
 Today we're introducing the H3 Family, which includes the flagship H3 and H3-Image series and a range of extended models. The flagship models and some of the extended models are available directly through the MiniMax Open Platform, MiniMax Code, and other platforms.
 We'll keep open-sourcing our work and supporting developers as they create with the H3 series. Together, we want to build one of the defining communities for multimodal creation in the AI era. Thank you to every user and developer for your trust, your ideas, and the work you've put in.
 Flagship models
 More power, more speed, and more variety 

 MiniMax H3
 Image quality comes first for high-end commercial productions
 2K or 768P output, starting at $0.08 per second

 You can tell H3 what you wish a scene would feel like, even when the idea is still hazy. It understands minimalist chic in an ad, a sense of mystery and fate in a film, or a traditional Chinese aesthetic in animation. It picks up on the difference between a character who is angry, one who feels wronged, and one who can't accept how things turned out. It can carry the story forward in a way that fits the characters and render it at up to 2K.
 H3 also follows instructions closely through a series of precise edits. You can replace a person, an item of clothing, or an object, or change the light and shadow in one part of a shot. The rest of the frame stays intact as you continue to refine it.

 MiniMax H3-Highspeed
 A first choice for everyday commercial work and large-scale production
 768P or 480P output, starting at $0.04 per second

 H3-Highspeed balances speed and cost with the quality and reliability that daily production demands. A five-second clip takes about 11 seconds to generate on average. For clips up to 15 seconds long, it takes no more than a minute to go from prompt to finished video. The model supports clips up to 30 seconds long.
 We worked with our partners to bring down inference costs. Teams in e-commerce, gaming, motion graphics, and live-action advertising often need a steady supply of material in large volumes. H3-Highspeed produces it in batches at a lower cost while maintaining quality. It's like having a colleague you can count on in your day-to-day work.

 MiniMax H3-Lite
 A lower-cost way to try out ideas
 768P or 480P output, starting at $0.025 per second

 On some hardware, H3-Lite generates a five-second 768P clip in about eight seconds. Video output for that clip costs about $0.20, with lower rates for high-volume use.
 H3-Lite's speed and low cost make it easier to work efficiently. Early in a project, you can test several ideas, compare the results, and choose a direction while spending less time and money. It's also well suited to producing repetitive commercial material in volume, such as dozens of ads showing the same product in different settings, or different characters acting out similar storylines.

 MiniMax H3-Image
 Fast generation, high image quality and close adherence to instructions
 Image output costs $3.80 per million tokens

 H3-Image produces high-quality images with rich detail and a realistic look. It follows instructions more closely than any other open-source model and supports up to 16 reference images. It also offers good value, generating images at 150% to 200% of the speed of mainstream models for 50% to 70% of the price.

 MiniMax H3-Image Highspeed
 4K images in 10 seconds
 Image output costs $7.60 per million tokens

 At its fastest, H3-Image in Highspeed mode can generate a 4K image in under 10 seconds. It runs 3 to 4 times as fast as mainstream models in the same tier, making 4K practical for everyday work.

 Extended models
 Options for specialized needs 

 fal: H3 Max
 Source: fal’s official website

 H3 Max generates a five-second 768P clip in under three seconds. Following prompts was a particular focus during training, so the model is both fast and responsive to what you ask for.

 fal: H3-Max Turbo
 Source: fal’s official website

 H3-Max Turbo generates video twice as fast as H3 Max. That opens up real-time interactions, virtual try-ons, continuous livestreams, and deduction games. There may be more you can do with it than we've thought of yet.

 MachGen: MiniMax H3
 Source: MachGen’s official website

 With MachGen's hosted model, you can preview results faster and use your own LoRAs to control the visual style.

 Nunchux AI: MiniMax-H3-Fast (Radical Speed)
 Source: Nunchux AI’s official website

 Nunchux AI optimized the model with hardware partners. It generates a 15-second 768P clip in about 6.5 seconds, making it well suited to creative work that takes several attempts and revisions.

 RunningHub: MiniMax H3 RH
 Source: RunningHub’s official website

 MiniMax H3 RH is well suited to short-form dramas, animated comic series, and e-commerce. Its strength is continuity. Even in a long, complex, unbroken shot, it keeps the character, setting, and story consistent.

 HeyGen: Video 1
 Source: HeyGen’s official website

 AI avatars are HeyGen's largest business, so the team places particular emphasis on applications such as e-commerce. Video 1 can generate a complete video up to 30 seconds long in one pass, including visuals, dialogue, and sound effects.

 Utopai Studios: Utopai X
 Source: Utopai Studios’ official website

 The team at Utopai Studios developed Utopai X inside a film studio specifically for filmmakers, with a focus on lighting and motion that obey the laws of physics.

 Suno: H3 Move
 Source: Suno’s official website

 Suno, a leading global AI music platform, developed H3 Move specifically for music videos. Working from a song's style and lyrics, it can produce a performance with lip movements matched precisely to the song, or a music video with an elegant, offbeat, ethereal, or modern look.

 Creatify: Boreal-H3
 Source: Creatify’s official website

 Creatify trained Boreal-H3 specifically for e-commerce ads. It pays particular attention to the product on screen, what it looks like in use, and the on-camera model's appearance and accent.

 Pika: H3 Flex
 Source: Pika’s official website

 Pika H3 Flex generates video in six steps and gives creators fine control over camera movement, action, and sound effects. It's well suited to people making ads or short films.

 LightX2V / ModelTC: MiniMax-H3 Turbo
 Source: LightX2V / ModelTC’s official model page

 MiniMax-H3 Turbo reduces video generation to four or eight steps and can run locally. It's designed for professionals who have their own deployment environment and want shorter waits.

 Akatz Labs: Character Swap V1.5
 Source: Akatz Labs’ official model page

 Akatz Labs built on H3 to improve its ability to replace just one part of a shot. Character Swap is designed primarily for one task. Choose the person to replace, and the reference character performs the original person's movements and expressions while the rest of the scene stays the same.

 These third-party and community models each have their own strengths and excel at particular tasks.
 All are available through the MiniMax Open Platform API. The H3 Family page features more examples of what people have built with H3.
